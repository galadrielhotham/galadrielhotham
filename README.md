# Galadriel Hotham

I’m a content creator and editorial organiser based in Sheffield, UK. I write business features, help contributors develop their ideas and plan publishing schedules that leave room for questions and checking.

I started in editorial support at Lantern Type Studio in 2018. Interview preparation and image selection taught me to look for the detail that helps a reader understand somebody’s everyday work. Through Street-Level Stories and Open Page Circle, I’ve kept that interest in local experience and useful conversation.

I’m interested in practical tools for community publishing, clear contributor guidance and manageable editorial calendars. I like small improvements that make it easier for people to share what they know.

Outside work, I photograph changing streets, read independent magazines and grow herbs in a small urban garden.
